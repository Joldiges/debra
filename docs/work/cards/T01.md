# T01 — measurable acceptance and safe test boundaries

Read T01, the complete brief, evidence rules, and roadmap gates. This is a test
design document, not implementation or execution of hardware/fault tests.

## Bounded questions

Define separate offline fixture/unit, image/privileged, and physical/fleet test
tiers. Cover identity/keys, bad Wi-Fi, no-login audio, HA wake/response, timers
versus alarms, announcements, music sync/rejoin, interruption/state preservation,
reboot/outage, storage exhaustion, updates, restore/rescue, and parent-facing UX.

## Acceptance

Map every product requirement to a test tier, observable pass/fail criterion,
required hardware/server/version evidence, recovery prerequisites, and report
fields. Label numeric latency/sync/stability thresholds as proposals with
rationale, not measurements or approved policy. Give safe fixture-test ideas
without inventing existing test commands. Include original Zero W full-capability
coverage and exact conditions under which a combination may be called supported.
