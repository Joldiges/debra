# R07 — alternative SBCs and real audio compatibility

Read R07 and the evidence rules for non-Pi boards. Research only; do not test
header wiring, flash media, install drivers, or commit Debra to a supported port.

## Bounded questions

Screen two currently documented Orange Pi models and at most one other low-cost
SBC. State why those exact models were selected; a brand is not a platform.
Start at official board manuals, schematics/pinouts, OS/kernel documentation,
then exact audio-expansion vendor/driver sources.

## Acceptance

For each board distinguish header/mounting fit, pin assignment, voltage/power,
I2S/I2C and clocks, driver/device-tree support, maintained OS/architecture, and
software dependencies. Evaluate Pi HAT reuse separately from board-native/USB
audio. Include complete usable cost, availability, onboarding/identity/recovery
implications and explicit unknowns. An unverified HAT match must not become
“compatible”; recommend exact next measurements/tests instead.
