# R02 — complete voice-device alternatives

Read R02, the brief's non-Pi boundary, and evidence rules. Research only; no
firmware updates, account access, purchases, or device operation.

## Bounded questions

Compare Home Assistant Voice Preview Edition, Atom Echo, and EchoS3R as distinct
products. Find exact vendor/upstream product and bring-up documentation. Compare
microphone/output hardware, power/accessories, wake/voice/timer/announcement
features, music and multiroom prerequisites, provider ownership, privacy,
maintenance, and full usable US/import cost.

## Acceptance

Provide a dated source-linked table, separating upstream-documented function,
third-party reports, unknowns, and Debra tests (`not-run`). Distinguish advertised
features from practical limitations and unsupported assumptions. Link upstream
setup paths instead of proposing Debra-owned firmware; do not treat countdown
timers as proof of persistent alarms.
