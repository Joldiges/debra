# R01 — affordable hardware inventory

Read the R01 roadmap row and hardware/cost sections of the brief and evidence
rules. Research only; no purchasing, device access, installation, or CAD.

## Bounded questions

- Cover original Zero W, Zero 2 W, and Pi 5 as development reference; ReSpeaker
  V1/V2 and WM8960 with exact product/revision distinctions.
- Separate microphone, amplifier, speaker, line/headphone output, controls,
  power, storage, cables, headers, and enclosure requirements.
- Use current vendor specifications and US seller/import listings. Date prices,
  record variants/quantities/shipping/tax assumptions; do not infer quality or
  compatibility from a listing.
- Present at least three cost tiers with separate complete-purchase and reused
  accessory totals. Investigate the ~$40 goal; a sourced failure to reach it is
  a valid finding.

## Acceptance

Each component has a primary specification/driver source or explicit `unknown`;
cost arithmetic is reproducible. Exact-revision compatibility is evidence-labeled,
not promised. Conclude with missing physical measurements/tests and a short
recommended next investigation. Do not edit shared buying recommendations.
