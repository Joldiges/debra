LAST STEP

Your phone is about to leave this network

This device has only one Wi-Fi radio, so it has to switch off the setup network before it can join your network. That will disconnect this page.

On your phone:

  • Reconnect to your home Wi-Fi.
  • Look for this device on that network under the name debra.
  • If the device could not join, it brings this setup network back and says why, so you can try again.

Nothing is rebooted. A join that fails comes back as soon as the attempt gives up; a join that works does not come back, because the device is on your network instead.

Once web control is installed, its own page is at http://debra.local:8787/
